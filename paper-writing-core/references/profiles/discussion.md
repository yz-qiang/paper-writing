# Discussion Profile

## Purpose

Interpret findings without presenting speculation as measured fact.

## Argument chain

```text
finding
→ supported interpretation
→ evidence for the interpretation
→ connection to prior work or broader implication
→ limitations of the interpretation
```

## Boundaries

Distinguish direct experimental evidence from inference. Mark plausible
mechanisms as interpretations unless the experiment isolates them. Do not use
Discussion to introduce a method component that Approach never defined.

## Audit

- Is the interpretation anchored to a reported finding?
- Does prior work support comparison rather than replace evidence?
- Are alternative explanations acknowledged when material?
- Do the stated limitations preserve the value of the supported conclusion?
