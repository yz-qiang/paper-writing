# Results Profile

## Purpose

Answer one research question with direct empirical evidence.

## Argument chain

```text
research question
→ measurement context
→ observation
→ quantitative evidence
→ bounded answer
```

Lead with the answer only when the metric and comparison context are already
clear. Otherwise establish the context first.

## Evidence discipline

- Report values with their baseline, unit, and aggregation rule.
- Separate the observation from an explanation of its mechanism.
- Move unsupported mechanism speculation to Discussion.
- Report negative and mixed results when they affect the bounded answer.
- Avoid repeating every table value in prose; state the pattern that answers
  the research question.

## Audit

- Does the paragraph answer the stated research question?
- Is every quantitative evidence claim traceable to a table, figure, or log?
- Is the answer no stronger than the evaluated subjects and workloads permit?
- Does Discussion own interpretations not directly tested by the experiment?
