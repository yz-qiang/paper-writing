# Empirical Study Profile

## Purpose

Explain how evidence is collected and converted into study findings or an
artifact used by later sections.

## Argument chain

```text
research question
→ study subjects
→ evidence collection
→ analysis procedure
→ measurement or coding rule
→ finding or artifact used by later sections
```

## Required distinctions

- Separate exploratory observations from predefined measurements.
- State the unit of analysis and aggregation rule.
- Distinguish a paper-level rule from its recurring forms and concrete cases.
- Separate rule discovery from later rule application.
- Identify artifacts frozen before evaluation, such as an optimization-card
  collection or taxonomy.

## Audit

- Can another researcher reconstruct how evidence became a finding?
- Is each research question answered by the reported measurement?
- Are counts tied to a clear denominator?
- Are later method claims limited to what the study established?
