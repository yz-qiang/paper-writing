# Threats to Validity Profile

## Purpose

State how a threat could affect the conclusion, what was done about it, and
what uncertainty remains.

## Argument unit

For each material threat, describe:

```text
threat source
→ possible influence on a claim
→ mitigation
→ remaining boundary
```

Organize threats by their relation to claims, not as a generic checklist.

## Boundaries

Do not claim that a mitigation eliminates a threat unless the design proves
that result. Avoid listing hypothetical threats with no plausible effect on the
paper's conclusions.

## Audit

- Is every threat connected to a specific conclusion?
- Does the mitigation describe an action actually taken?
- Is remaining uncertainty explicit?
- Are generalizability limits consistent with the evaluated subjects?
